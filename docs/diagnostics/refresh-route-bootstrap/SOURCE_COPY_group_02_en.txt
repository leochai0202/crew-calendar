Common procedures: RNAV; PDC sometimes assigns the SAPUT-8WD departure, with the route proceeding to TEBON after SAPUT.

For departures from Runway 04 towards DABER, the tower may temporarily instruct the aircraftto maintain the runway heading after takeoff.
