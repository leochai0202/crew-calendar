JIEYANG CHAOSHAN Airport:

Ground control/dispatch frequency: 130.25.

ATC instructions：.

After landing on Runway 22, ATC may instruct the aircraft to hold short of A and L; note that there are no painted hold-short lines on the ground—exercise caution to avoid conflicts with aircraft taxiing out via Taxiway L.

For the ILS approach to Runway 22: When arriving via IAF OW556, terrain effects may result in a higher-than-normal profile, potentially leading to a high intercept; it is recommended to be aware of this and are advised to extend flaps to Configuration 2 before turning final approach.and crew may also request descend 700 meters to avoid high interception.

Jeppesen charts do not include arrival or departure procedures for the star from P319; please mention this when operating with foreign flight crews.

For the taxi route Y2-T1-J, ATC does not state "T1" in the taxi clearance.

Runway slope (elevation difference > 10m): Runway 04 threshold elevation is 15.6m; Runway 22 threshold elevation is 5.1m. A noticeable downslope exists, particularly when landing on Runway 04; it is recommended to exercise caution.

180-degree turns on the runway or taxiways are prohibited.

Prevailing wind is East-Southeast (ESE); approaches often involve tailwinds. Thunderstorms and tropical cyclones are frequent in summer. Be alert to the effects of turbulence.
